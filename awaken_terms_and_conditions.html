4. Description of the Service

Awaken is an alarm clock application designed to help you wake up consistently. Its central feature is that a scheduled alarm continues to sound until you complete a "mission" — a short task that requires you to be genuinely awake and, in most cases, physically out of bed.

Missions currently include photo-based tasks (for example, photographing a made bed, the sky, a pet, or a randomly assigned household object), spoken tasks (reciting a verse or speaking affirmations aloud), a motion task (shaking the device a set number of times), and an arithmetic task.

Photo and spoken missions are verified using artificial intelligence, as described in Section 6. Motion and arithmetic missions are verified entirely on your device and require no internet connection.

Additional features include alarm scheduling and repetition, selectable and user-imported alarm sounds, streak tracking, and progress insights. We may add, modify, or remove features over time in accordance with Section 14.

6. AI Mission Verification

Photo and spoken missions are verified using Google's Gemini artificial intelligence models, accessed through Firebase AI. When you complete such a mission, the photograph or audio recording you capture is transmitted to Google for the sole purpose of determining whether the mission was satisfied. A result is returned to your device and the alarm stops if the mission passed.

We do not store, retain, or have access to your photographs or audio recordings. Captured audio is deleted from your device immediately after verification, and photographs are held only in temporary device memory for the duration of the attempt. Full details are set out in our Privacy Policy.

AI verification is probabilistic and may be wrong. A valid attempt may occasionally be rejected, and an invalid one may occasionally be accepted. We do not warrant the accuracy of any verification result. If a mission cannot be completed, the App provides an emergency override that silences the alarm without completing the mission.

AI-verified missions require an active internet connection. If your device is offline, the App will inform you that verification cannot be performed; you may then use the emergency override or select an on-device mission such as shake or arithmetic.
