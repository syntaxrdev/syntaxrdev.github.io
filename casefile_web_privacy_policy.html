Privacy Policy

CaseFile on the web · Nex Atlas Labs · Last updated: October 13, 2026

In short: you can play Case One without an account. If you sign in and subscribe, we keep what we need to run your account and subscription: your Google name, email and profile photo, and your subscription status. Payments are handled by Paddle; we never see your card details. We do not sell your data, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

1. Who we are

CaseFile on the web at casefile-game.web.app (the "Service") is provided by Nex Atlas Labs ("we," "us"), the data controller for the information described here. The CaseFile mobile apps have their own Privacy Policy.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information (only if you sign in). When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address, profile photo and a unique account identifier, through Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Subscription information. Your plan, subscription status, renewal or end date, and the customer and subscription identifiers Paddle gives us, so we can unlock Premium for your account.
  • Referral information. If you use the invite feature: your invite code, which accounts accepted your invites, whether those friends have paid, and the rewards earned. Friends you invite can see that they came from your invite.
  • Profile. The detective name you choose in the game, if any.
  • Technical information. Like any website, our hosting and storage providers receive your IP address, browser type and request logs when you load the Service, its videos and images.
  • Stored on your device only. Game progress, language, sound and similar settings are kept in your browser's local storage and are not sent to us.

We do not collect payment card details, precise location, contacts or photos, and we do not use analytics, advertising or cross-site tracking.

3. How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account and Premium access working (performance of our contract with you).
  • To run the referral program and grant rewards you earn.
  • To provide support and answer your requests.
  • To keep the Service secure and prevent fraud and abuse (our legitimate interests).
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

4. Payments: Paddle

Purchases are processed by Paddle.com, our reseller and Merchant of Record. Paddle collects the details needed for payment, tax and fraud prevention (such as your name, email, country, postcode and payment method) under its own Privacy Policy. Paddle tells us that a payment or subscription succeeded, renewed or ended, plus the identifiers listed above; it does not share your card details with us. Paddle may also use its Paddle Retain service to help recover failed renewal payments.

5. Service providers

  • Google (Firebase): sign-in, website hosting, database and media storage.
  • Paddle: checkout, payments, tax, invoicing and subscription management.

These providers process data on our behalf or as independent controllers under their own terms, and may process it in countries other than yours with appropriate safeguards. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

6. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. The Service uses your browser's local storage for game progress and settings, and keeps you signed in. Google sign-in and the Paddle checkout set cookies that those services need to work.

7. How long we keep it

We keep account, subscription and referral information while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within 30 days, except for records we (or Paddle) must keep for tax, accounting or legal reasons. Local game data stays in your browser until you clear it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict how we use it. To use any of these rights, including deleting your account, email us from the address linked to your account. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), access to your account data is limited to your own signed-in account, and subscription records can only be written by our servers after a verified payment notification.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date above, and tell you in the Service before a significant change takes effect.
