Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Overview

GyroMaze is designed as a privacy-first experience. The app does not collect, store, or share personal data.

Gyroscope Usage

The app uses device motion data including gyroscope and accelerometer input strictly for gameplay controls.

This data is processed in real time on your device and is never stored, transmitted, or shared.

Data Collection

GyroMaze does not collect any personal information such as name, email, location, or contacts.

No analytics, tracking systems, or third party data collection services are used.

Data Storage

All game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

No data is uploaded to external servers.

Permissions

The app may request access to motion sensors for gameplay purposes.

No other sensitive permissions are required.

Third Party Services

GyroMaze does not integrate with any third party services that collect user data.

Children Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any data from users of any age.

Changes

This policy may be updated in future versions. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact

If you have questions regarding this policy, contact support through the app listing.
