Privacy Policy

BodyTimeline – 30 Day Transformation

Last updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how BodyTimeline – 30 Day Transformation ("the App") handles information when you use the application. The app is developed and published by Syntaxr.

1. Information We Collect

BodyTimeline is designed to respect user privacy. The app does not collect personal information such as names, emails, or contact information.

The app allows users to record body transformation progress such as:

  • Progress photos
  • Body measurements
  • Nutrition logs
  • Fitness progress data

All such data is stored locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to external servers.

2. Local Data Storage

BodyTimeline stores user generated data locally on the device using secure device storage. This may include photos taken within the app and measurement data entered by the user.

This information is never uploaded to external servers unless the user explicitly chooses to share exported media outside the app.

3. Subscriptions and Payments

BodyTimeline offers optional premium features through auto-renewable subscriptions available via Apple's App Store.

Payments are processed securely by Apple. The developer does not collect or store payment information such as credit card numbers or billing details.

Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period. Users can manage or cancel subscriptions through their Apple ID account settings.

4. Data Usage

The app uses the stored information solely to provide its intended functionality, including:

  • Tracking body transformation progress
  • Displaying visual comparisons of progress photos
  • Providing historical tracking of measurements and nutrition logs

The developer does not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

5. Third Party Services

BodyTimeline may rely on Apple's system services for purchases and device functionality. These services operate under Apple's privacy policies.

For more information, please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect data stored within the application. Since data is stored locally on the device, users maintain control over their information.

Deleting the application from the device will permanently remove the stored data associated with the app.

7. Children's Privacy

BodyTimeline is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Users are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
