Privacy Policy

BodyTimeline – 30 Day Transformation

Last updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the application BodyTimeline – 30 Day Transformation ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the mobile application. The App is developed and published by Syntaxr.

Protecting user privacy is extremely important to us. This policy describes what information may be generated while using the application, how that information is handled, and how users maintain control over their personal data.

1. Overview of Data Practices

BodyTimeline is designed as a privacy-focused application. The app does not require account creation and does not collect personally identifiable information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, or government identifiers.

All user-generated information remains stored locally on the user's device unless the user explicitly chooses to export or share data outside of the application.

2. Information Created or Stored by the User

While using the App, users may create and store certain types of personal fitness tracking information. This information may include:

  • Progress photos
  • Body measurements
  • Nutrition or macronutrient logs
  • Fitness progress tracking data
  • Transformation timeline records

This information is created by the user solely for personal progress tracking and is not transmitted to external servers.

3. Face Data and Photos

The application allows users to capture or upload progress photos. These photos may include images of the user's face as part of full-body transformation tracking.

The application does not perform facial recognition, facial scanning, biometric identification, or automated analysis of facial features. Photos are used solely to allow the user to visually compare physical progress over time.

The app does not extract biometric identifiers, does not analyze facial geometry, and does not attempt to identify individuals within images.

Images remain private to the user and are stored only on the user’s device unless the user manually exports or shares them.

4. Local Storage of Data

All photos, measurements, nutrition logs, and progress statistics are stored locally on the user’s device within the application's private storage directory.

This storage location is protected by the operating system’s sandboxing and security protections. The application does not upload, transmit, or synchronize this information to remote servers.

Because the data is stored locally, users maintain complete control over their information.

5. Data Sharing

BodyTimeline does not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share user data with third parties.

User-generated data is never transmitted to external services except when the user explicitly chooses to share exported content through the device’s sharing features.

6. Data Retention

All information created within the application remains stored locally on the user's device until one of the following actions occurs:

  • The user deletes the data from within the application
  • The user removes the application from the device

Once the application is deleted, all associated locally stored data is permanently removed from the device.

7. Subscriptions and Payments

BodyTimeline offers optional premium features through auto-renewable subscriptions available via Apple's App Store.

All payments are processed securely by Apple through the Apple In-App Purchase system. The developer does not collect or store payment information such as credit card numbers or billing addresses.

Subscription management, billing, and cancellation are handled directly through the user’s Apple ID account settings.

8. Third-Party Services

The App may rely on Apple platform services such as the App Store for subscription processing and device operating system features for secure data storage.

These services are governed by Apple’s own privacy policies and terms of service.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information stored within the application. Data stored locally on the device benefits from the security protections provided by the operating system, including application sandboxing and device-level security controls.

However, users are responsible for maintaining the security of their own devices.

10. Children's Privacy

BodyTimeline is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
