Cellz Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

Cellz is developed and published by Syntaxr. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

Information We Collect

Cellz does not collect, store, or share any personal information from users.

The game operates entirely offline and does not require account creation.

Gameplay Data

The game may store basic gameplay statistics locally on your device, such as:

  • Level progress
  • Wins and losses
  • Scores

This information is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to any server.

Audio Permissions

The application plays sound effects through the device speaker. The app does not record audio or access the microphone.

Children’s Privacy

Cellz does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Services

Cellz does not use third-party analytics or advertising services that collect personal user data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Updates will be reflected on this page.
