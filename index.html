About Syntaxr

Syntaxr is a brand dedicated to delivering engaging and thoughtful digital experiences. Our focus is on creating intuitive applications and games that combine performance, strategy, and polished user interfaces.

Our flagship puzzle game Cellz challenges players to connect dots, form squares, and outmaneuver opponents through strategy and precision. Players can challenge intelligent AI or compete with friends locally on the same device.

At Syntaxr we prioritize clean design, reliable performance, and enjoyable gameplay.
