Refund Policy

CaseFile on the web · Nex Atlas Labs · Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy covers CaseFile Premium subscriptions bought on the web at casefile-game.web.app. Subscriptions bought in the iOS or Android app are refunded by Apple or Google under their own policies.

1. 14-day refunds

If you are not happy with CaseFile Premium, you can request a full refund within 14 days of any payment, whether it was your first purchase or a renewal (weekly or yearly). No reason is needed.

2. How to request a refund

Our orders are processed by our reseller Paddle.com, the Merchant of Record, which handles refunds. To request one, either:

  • use the link in your payment receipt email, or visit paddle.net and look up your order; or
  • email us at support@nexatlaslabs.com with the email address you paid with, and we will arrange it.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Banks usually take 5–10 business days to show the credit.

3. After a refund

When a payment is refunded, the subscription it paid for is cancelled and Premium access ends.

4. Cancelling instead

You can cancel at any time to stop future renewals, using the link in your receipt email or at paddle.net. Cancelling does not refund the current period; you keep Premium until it ends. To get money back, request a refund as described above.

5. After 14 days

Payments older than 14 days are not normally refundable, except where required by law, or if the Service is discontinued or we end your access without a breach of our Terms of Service on your part, in which case we refund the unused part of your paid period. If you were charged by mistake or something went wrong, contact us anyway: we look at every request.

6. Your statutory rights

This policy does not affect any rights you have under the consumer protection laws of your country.
