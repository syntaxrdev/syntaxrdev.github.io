Privacy Policy

Carbon Footprint – C-Footprint
Package: com.uetmds.c_footprint
Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the application Carbon Footprint ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application. The App is developed and published by the UETM DS team.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Please read this policy carefully to understand our practices.

1. Information We Collect

The App collects the following types of information:

a) Account Information

When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we receive your Google account's public profile information, which includes your display name, email address, and profile photo URL. This information is used solely to identify your account within the App.

b) Activity Logs

The App allows you to log carbon-emitting activities. The data you enter or that is automatically captured may include:

  • Food consumption entries (meal type, quantity)
  • Travel activities (transport type, distance, speed)
  • Electricity usage (kWh values)
  • Smart tracking sessions (GPS coordinates, travel distance, step count, movement mode, timestamps)

This data is stored in your personal Firebase Firestore database under your authenticated user account and is not accessible to other users.

c) Location Data

The Smart Tracking feature uses your device's GPS to track travel distance and detect movement mode (walking or vehicle). Location data is used in real time to calculate carbon emissions and is stored as part of your activity logs. Location tracking only occurs while the feature is actively enabled by you.

On Android, background location access may be requested to continue tracking when the app is not in the foreground. This access is used exclusively for the Smart Tracking feature and is never used for advertising or shared with third parties.

d) Motion and Activity Data

The App accesses your device's motion sensors and step counter to detect walking activity and calculate step counts as part of Smart Tracking sessions.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the App's core features (carbon tracking, smart tracking, insights)
  • To sync your activity data across sessions via Firebase Firestore
  • To calculate and display your personal carbon footprint statistics
  • To send local notifications about tracking session results and daily target alerts
  • To display your name and profile photo within the App's profile screen

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering the App's features.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your activity data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore under your personal user account (identified by your Firebase UID). Access is restricted by Firebase Security Rules so that only you can read or write your own data.

We rely on Google Firebase's infrastructure for data security. Firebase applies industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties for commercial purposes. Your data may be processed by the following service providers solely to operate the App:

  • Google Firebase – Authentication, cloud database (Firestore), and cloud storage
  • Google Sign-In – Account authentication

These providers operate under their own privacy policies and applicable data protection laws.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your activity data is retained in Firestore as long as your account exists. You may delete individual activity logs from within the App at any time. To request full deletion of your account and all associated data, contact us at the email address below and we will process the request within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

7. Permissions Used

The App requests the following device permissions:

  • Location (foreground and background) – For Smart Tracking distance calculation
  • Activity Recognition / Motion – For step counting and movement detection
  • Notifications – For tracking session summaries and daily target alerts
  • Internet – For Firebase authentication and data sync

All permissions are requested only when relevant features are used and can be revoked at any time via your device settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
