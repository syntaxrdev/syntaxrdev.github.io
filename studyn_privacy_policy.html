Studyn Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Studyn collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application and related services. By using Studyn, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Important: Studyn is an AI-powered study and note-taking app. Because the app lets users upload documents, audio, images, notes, and other study materials, we process this content only to provide app features such as notes, summaries, chat, quizzes, file previews, cloud sync, and document retrieval.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you sign in, we may collect basic account information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Profile image URL
  • User ID generated by authentication providers
  • Sign-in provider information, such as Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In

1.2 User Content

Studyn may process content that you create, upload, or save inside the app, including:

  • Notes and markdown content
  • PDF files
  • Word documents and other document files
  • Images
  • Audio recordings
  • Video files or video links
  • Website links and metadata
  • YouTube links and thumbnails
  • Generated quizzes, summaries, flashcards, and AI responses

1.3 AI Interaction Data

When you use AI features, we may process:

  • Your prompts and questions
  • Relevant note or document context
  • AI-generated responses
  • Document references used to generate answers

1.4 Files and Storage Data

When you upload or record content, we may store metadata such as:

  • File name
  • File type
  • File size
  • Storage path
  • Download URL
  • Upload and processing status
  • Error messages or processing status codes

1.5 Usage and Technical Information

We may collect technical information needed to operate and improve the app, including:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • Crash logs and diagnostic data
  • Authentication state
  • Feature usage events
  • Network or sync status

1.6 Subscription and Purchase Information

If you purchase a subscription, payment processing is handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not directly collect or store your full payment card details. We may receive subscription status information through payment and subscription management services such as RevenueCat, including whether your subscription is active, expired, cancelled, or in trial.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Store and sync notes across devices
  • Upload, preview, and open files
  • Generate AI summaries, notes, quizzes, flashcards, and answers
  • Process documents for search and retrieval
  • Provide subscription access and premium features
  • Improve app stability, performance, and user experience
  • Detect and fix errors, crashes, and sync issues
  • Protect the app from abuse or unauthorized access

3. AI Processing

Studyn uses AI systems to help users understand and study their uploaded content. This may require sending selected text, document chunks, notes, prompts, or file-derived content to AI processing services.

AI-generated content may be inaccurate or incomplete. You should review important outputs before relying on them for exams, assignments, professional work, or decisions.

4. How We Store Your Data

Studyn may store data using secure cloud services such as Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Storage. Files may also be cached locally on your device to allow faster opening and previewing.

Data Type Storage Location Purpose
Account data Authentication/database services Login, profile, and account management
Notes and markdown Cloud database Syncing notes across devices
Uploaded files Cloud storage and local cache Previewing, retrieval, and AI processing
Subscription status RevenueCat / platform billing systems Premium access verification
AI prompts and responses App backend / AI processing services Generating study assistance

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only with service providers needed to operate Studyn, such as:

  • Authentication providers
  • Cloud database and storage providers
  • AI processing providers
  • Subscription and purchase management providers
  • Crash reporting or analytics providers, if enabled

These providers process information only as needed to provide their services to Studyn.

6. Third-Party Services

Studyn may rely on third-party services, including but not limited to:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Storage
  • RevenueCat
  • Apple App Store billing
  • Google Play billing, if Android is supported
  • AI model providers for text, document, or speech processing

7. Subscriptions and Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not directly receive or store your payment card information. Subscription status may be processed through RevenueCat to determine whether premium features should be unlocked.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no internet-based service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials and device secure.

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide Studyn, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and maintain backups. User-generated notes and files may remain stored until you delete them or request deletion of your account, subject to technical backup retention periods.
