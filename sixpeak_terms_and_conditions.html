6. AI Analysis & Coach Chat

Physique scans and AI coach replies are generated using OpenAI's models, accessed through our backend. When you run a scan or send a message, the photo or message is transmitted to our backend, which forwards it to OpenAI for the sole purpose of producing your result. A response is returned to your device and, in the case of scans and confirmed nutrition entries, saved to your local history.

We do not retain your photographs or chat messages on our backend. They are processed to produce a result and are not written to a database or disk we control. Full details are set out in our Privacy Policy.

AI analysis is probabilistic and may be inaccurate. A physique score, coach reply, or suggested nutrition entry may occasionally be wrong, incomplete, or nonsensical. We do not warrant the accuracy of any AI-generated result. You are responsible for reviewing any suggestion — including a proposed nutrition entry — before you rely on it or confirm it into your log.

AI-verified features require an active internet connection. If your device is offline, the App will inform you that a scan or chat message cannot be processed; on-device features such as exercise form tracking remain unaffected.
